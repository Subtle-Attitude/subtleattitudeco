Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Thank you for shopping with Subtle Attitude LLC. We want you to love your journals.

Returns Accepted For:

  • Damaged items upon arrival (torn cover, defective binding, printing errors)
  • Incorrect item sent (wrong design or version on our end)

Timeframe:

Contact us within 14 days of delivery with photos and your order number.

Not Eligible:

  • Change of mind
  • Used or written-in journals
  • Items showing signs of wear

Process:

  1. Message us at contact@subtleattitude.co with order details + photos.
  2. Do not ship back until approved.
  3. Return in original packaging (you pay return shipping unless our error).
  4. Refund issued within 5–7 business days after inspection (original shipping non-refundable).

International orders: Buyer responsible for all return shipping and customs.
